About the role

Join Ericsson Antenna Systems in Rosenheim, Germany as a Strategic Product Manager. You will own antenna product strategy and lifecycle from concept through end-of-life, ensuring solutions exceed market expectations and drive commercial value. Partner closely with R&D, sales and cross-functional teams to define solutions, prioritize roadmaps and prepare the business for market launches. You will also build competence and readiness across the organization while balancing dependencies and stakeholder needs. This role suits a commercially-minded, senior product leader with deep telecom domain expertise.

What you will do:

Own and manage the antenna product portfolio end-to-end, including lifecycle and EOL decisions
Define product solutions with R&D, create go-to-market plans and drive successful launches
Work with sales and customers to translate market and operator requirements into product specifications
Coordinate cross-functional activities across supply chain, development, sales and marketing to ensure business readiness
Monitor market and competitor trends to inform prioritization and strategic decisions
Develop and share competence across teams to strengthen product and solution knowledge
Support sales with product positioning, demonstrations and technical input as needed
The skills you bring:

Master’s degree in Telecommunications, RF Engineering or a closely related field
Senior experience in Radio Access Network, mobile communications, antennas, 4G/5G and RF domains
Strong product management know-how and experience managing roadmaps, lifecycles and go-to-market activities
Commercial and entrepreneurial mindset with excellent presentation and communication skills
Proven ability to engage stakeholders, drive decisions and work effectively under pressure
Self-driven, organized and able to capture both the big picture and technical details
Experience in antenna or radio product management, development or sales and familiarity with operator projects is a strong plus
